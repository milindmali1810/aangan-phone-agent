# Label these 10 enquiries

Open `qualified.md`, `services.md` and `rubric.md` beside this. For each one, write:

- **Label:** FORWARD, FORWARD_WITH_FLAG or DECLINE
- **Why:** one line, naming which criterion decided it

Rules of thumb from our rubric: out of scope or far-too-low volunteered budget or under 6 weeks = DECLINE; unclear decision-maker or borderline budget or 6-10 weeks = FORWARD_WITH_FLAG; everything fine = FORWARD. Asking for a price never disqualifies.

Note: some of these are web-form or WhatsApp records, not live calls. Judge the content the same way.
